Privacy Policy for Surya Agencies

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Surya Agencies ordering application, we collect basic details necessary to prepare and fulfill your ice cream and dairy pickup orders:

  • Contact Information: Your name and mobile phone number for order identification and pickup verification.
  • Order Details: The dairy and ice cream products selected, quantities, and chosen payment method (UPI / Pay at Shop).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Prepare your ordered dairy and ice cream items for counter pickup at our parlour.
  • Provide live real-time status updates on your order ticket.
  • Assist the shopkeeper with order fulfillment and stock management.

3. Payment & Data Security

We do NOT store your bank details, credit/debit card numbers, or UPI PINs. All UPI payments are processed directly through your device's banking/UPI application (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) with standard bank-grade encryption.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third-party advertisers or external data brokers.
